# PrimoShop

> Public project brief · Source code: Private

## Overview

A full-stack commerce platform that brings together catalog discovery, orders, content operations, reporting, and secure administration.

## Public scope

- Customer-facing catalog and purchase workflows
- Administrative content and order operations
- Business reporting and external service integrations

## Selected technologies

React, TypeScript, Express, Prisma, Cloudinary, and PostgreSQL-compatible persistence.
