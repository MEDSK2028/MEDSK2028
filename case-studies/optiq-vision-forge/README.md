# OPTIQ Vision Forge

> Public project brief · Source code: Private

## Overview

An optical-commerce experience that combines product discovery, interactive visual tools, content operations, and full-stack administration.

## Public scope

- Customer-facing product discovery and commerce flows
- Interactive visualization and assisted product selection
- Persistent catalog, content, order, and administrative workflows

## Selected technologies

React, TypeScript, MediaPipe, Three.js, Express, PostgreSQL, and Cloudinary.
